How we choose our picks

We do not run lab tests or time trials in our own office. Instead, our recommendations are built from a careful review of long-term owner feedback across multiple retail and review platforms, combined with the published evaluations of established experts and detailed teardowns of what actually fails. We look at reputation: which brands have a consistent track record of making stands that do not break, scratch jewelry, or shed material. What matters most over time are the hinges, the felt or velvet lining, the stability of the base, and whether earring posts stay secure or necklaces tangle. Simple mechanisms often last longer than overly complex ones, and we favor designs that have held up well in real homes, not just on launch day. We also consider how easy a stand is to clean, whether it collects dust, and if it truly fits the jewelry sizes people actually own.
